      And everything else

      Everything your store runs on.

      Catalog, promotions, exchanges, emails, invoices and your storefront — in the same calm admin.

      • Catalog & combos

        Products, combos, categories, and collections — with sizes and SKUs for every variant.

      • Promotions & codes

        Multibuy offers like “Buy 2, get free delivery,” plus discount codes for every campaign.

      • Exchanges

        Customers request exchanges within a window you set. Track each one to the end.

      • Reviews & feedback

        Moderate product reviews and read customer feedback in one place.

      • Product requests

        See which products your customers are asking you to stock next.

      • Offer & delivery emails

        Send a promotion to every active customer, and keep buyers posted on delivery.
